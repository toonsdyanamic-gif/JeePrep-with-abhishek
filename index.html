About The Trust

Kailash Founding Trust Private Limited is a newly established welfare organization formed with a mission to uplift underserved communities. Operating out of Rourkela, Odisha, we strive to build sustainable infrastructure that offers education, housing, and life-saving medical aid.

Our Core Initiatives

Schools for Underprivileged

Establishing free primary and secondary schools to ensure that poverty never stops a child from gaining knowledge and securing a bright future.

Old Age Homes

Building secure, comfortable homes for senior citizens, offering medical care, shelter, and companionship for their golden years.

Hospital Chains

Setting up affordable and charitable hospital facilities in underserved areas to provide quality treatments and emergency care.

About The Founder

Abhishek Sahoo

Founder & Managing Director

Abhishek Sahoo founded Kailash Founding Trust Private Limited with a vision to systematically eliminate social inequality through education, shelter, and healthcare infrastructure. Driven by a deep commitment to social welfare, his goal is to bridge the gap for underprivileged families and senior citizens across Odisha and beyond.

Under his leadership, the trust is establishing foundational programs aimed at sustainable, long-term impact.
